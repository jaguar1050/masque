Project MASQUE

Open screening instrument and research tooling for migraine- and neuropathy-masquerading ENT presentations.

  • release 0.3.1
  • instrument 0.2
  • lexicon 0.3.1
  • probe set 1.0.0

Prototype · not for clinical use Calibration constants are illustrative until fit on approved data. Nothing here has been validated in a clinical study. Each app repeats this on its own face rather than relying on this page.

Clinician Screener →

Six steps behind a mandatory safety gate, with FHIR write-back and pilot cohort capture. The reference implementation of the instrument.

Ambient Scribe →

Extracts findings from encounter speech — scripted, typed, or live from the microphone — prompts for the high-yield gaps, and drafts a note. Probes are ranked by what the answer could change.

Patient Companion →

Produces a visit-preparation summary. No score, no band, no probability and no research capture — each omission is deliberate.

Simulator →

Interactive walkthrough of all four apps, carrying an inline copy of the probe set so it runs standalone.

The screener and scribe both embed the shared ResearchReadinessPanel — cohort ingestion, validation, calibration, fairness audit and model card.

Served as source: the .jsx modules are compiled in your browser, so what runs here is byte-for-byte what is in the 0.3.1 methods repository. First load of an app compiles a few hundred KB of JSX and takes a moment.
